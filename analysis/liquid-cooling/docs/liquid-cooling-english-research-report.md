## 6. Key Takeaways for Competitive Analysis

1. **Market Size Consensus (2024-2025):** Liquid cooling hardware $1-5B; broader market (incl. services) $5-7B; growing at 20-40% CAGR
2. **Market Leaders (Thermal Management):** Vertiv #1 globally; Schneider Electric strong in CDU; Delta Electronics emerging as AI infrastructure backbone
3. **Taiwan Supply Chain Dominance:** AVC, Auras, Delta, Cooler Master are **NVIDIA's certified supply chain** for GB200/GB300 liquid cooling
4. **OCP Standardization:** Stäubli UQD is the OCP standard for quick disconnects; CPC dominates with >40% share
5. **Technology Shift:** Direct-to-chip (cold plate) dominates at 43% share; immersion fastest growing at 27% CAGR
6. **Adoption Inflection:** Gartner predicts 90% of new DCs will use liquid cooling by 2030 — massive TAM expansion
7. **Capacity Constraints:** Omdia notes production capacity (CDUs) was main growth constraint in 2023, not demand
